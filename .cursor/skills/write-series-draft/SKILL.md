# Write series draft

Story only. No fal. No `series.json`. No prompts. Stop after the draft and wait.

If the user has not approved the last depth, do not invent the next one. Do not write a full tree up front.

## First scene

A good first scene puts the hero in the situation and ends on someone offering or demanding a move. Face in frame. Situation already obvious. No travel, no lore, no second plot.

## Choices

Good choices are two different things the hero does right now, to someone or at someone. You can see both land. Both stay in the situation. Neither is looking, waiting, or leaving.

## How to draft

1. Name the situation in one line. Who invited the hero, where, why they are wanted.
2. Write episode `0` as one beat that ends on the offer.
3. Write two choice labels. Each is a verb you can film in the next second.
4. Write only the two child beats (`0a`, `0b`). What happens when that act lands. Stop.

Ids stay `0`, `0a`, `0b`, then `0aa` / `0ab` only after the user wants the next depth.

## Dead on arrival

Rewrite the choice if it:

- names a feeling instead of a move
- is a menu of activities
- leaves the situation
- is the same scene twice with different blocking
- is leftover from the last beat (the drink is gone; do not fork the last two drops)
